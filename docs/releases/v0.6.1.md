# lnwdeck v0.6.1

This release makes the pet livelier and the hover quota easier to use.

## Highlights

- **Random ambient poses.** While idle the pet now randomly plays the
  spritesheet poses instead of only standing still: waving, jumping, waiting,
  reviewing and looking left/right, before resuming its walk.
- **Scrollable hover quota.** The hover tooltip keeps the mouse wheel and
  stays open while the cursor is over it, so a long list of quota windows can
  be scrolled through comfortably.
- **Drag stops the walk.** Pressing and holding the pet cancels the movement
  phase immediately and settles it to idle, so dragging never fights the
  walking animation. Normal behaviour resumes after release.
